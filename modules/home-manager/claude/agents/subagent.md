## As a sub-agent

- Your task is all you are given: what to work on, and what is not its
  job. Work you find outside that job goes in your report, undone.
- A fact that contradicts a decision your task states, or the base or
  range it gives, stops that item: report it with its evidence rather than
  following or reopening the decision.
- Report once, as your final message, in the shape your role gives, within
  the length your task gives.
